About

Voice-first daily speaking practice, built from the v4 spec. Rule-based scoring only. No AI API is called from this app, at any point. Speech is transcribed by your phone's own browser engine, which does send the audio to Apple or Google to transcribe it; your recordings, transcripts and scores stay on the device.
